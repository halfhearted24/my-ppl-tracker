Progression rule: Record actual reps for every set. Next time, aim for one more rep per set if technique stays good and you still have 1–3 reps in reserve. Once all sets reach the top of the range, increase resistance slightly and return to the lower end.

Weekly schedule

Fixed weekly sequence: Monday Push A through Saturday Legs B; Sunday is always Recovery. Sessions are designed for about 45 minutes including a brief warm-up. Pair non-competing accessory exercises where practical; rest 60–90 seconds after most sets and 90–120 seconds after demanding compound sets.

12-week training progression

Weeks 1–3Learn form and establish baseline
Week 4Deload: reduce sets by around 30–40%
Weeks 5–7Build reps or resistance gradually
Week 8Deload and review recovery
Weeks 9–11Continue progressive overload
Week 12Review progress and technique

Deload weeks: keep the exercises, reduce working sets, and finish comfortably away from failure. Do not force progression when fatigued or sore.

Equipment notes: Equipment: adjustable incline bench, straight barbell and EZ bar with limited plates, 5 kg dumbbells, and door-anchor resistance bands. No squat rack: do not perform barbell back squats or incline barbell bench presses. Use a safe setup for floor press, RDL and hip thrust. Start lateral and rear-delt work with light band tension; stop for shoulder pain.

Backup your tracker

V5 saves to this browser's local storage and also attempts a second copy in IndexedDB. Saving is triggered as you type. Export a JSON backup after each workout and before clearing browser data, changing browsers, or changing phones.

Storage and offline use

Install this app from its HTTPS address using Samsung Internet’s Add page to → Home screen option. After the first visit, the app shell is cached for offline use. Workout data stays in this browser on this device; browser storage can still be cleared by cleanup tools, so export backups regularly.

Your training data stays on this device unless you export and share a backup. V5 checks the old V4 storage key and migrates records when that storage is accessible.
